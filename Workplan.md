# PIZZA RECIPE
## Made by: Group 5

### 1. Prepare the dough
- Add water, flour and salt and mix altogether

### 2. Tomato sauce
- Spread your favorite tomato sauce all over the dough

### 3. Add toppings
- Sprinkle mozzarella cheese
- Add mushrooms, pitted black olives, onions, tomatoes and basil

### 4. Bake pizza
- Set the oven to 250, bake pizza until finished. 

### 5. Enjoy pizza!
